Version 1.2.1 fixes missing NVIDIA GPU setup in the new Windows desktop app.

- Windows now asks you to choose CPU or NVIDIA GPU training before the first build.
- Use **Training device…** in Bank settings to change your choice later, including after upgrading from v1.2.0.
- NVIDIA setup downloads CUDA-enabled PyTorch into your user-data folder and verifies GPU availability and a real CUDA allocation before enabling it. A compatible NVIDIA driver and several GB of download/storage space are required; no separate CUDA toolkit is needed.
- Your choice is remembered. CPU training remains available, and failed or cancelled setup preserves the previous choice and bundled CPU runtime.
- CUDA setup and a one-batch training/export test were verified locally on an RTX 3060. Portable CPU training, relocation checks and repository regressions also passed.

Extract the complete portable ZIP into a new folder, then run `nam2zoom.exe` on Windows. Do not mix files from different releases. If you previously chose CPU, open **Training device…** to enable NVIDIA acceleration.

> [!WARNING]
> **The macOS version remains untested on real hardware, including USB-MIDI transfers.** macOS training uses CPU; Apple GPU acceleration (MPS) remains disabled. The macOS app is unsigned and unnotarized. Successful CI checks do not establish safe pedal transfers or reliable hardware operation.

Before testing on a pedal, keep full backup enabled, disable autosave and select a stock patch. Test N2Z alone in an unsaved patch at low monitoring volume before adding other effects. Saved-patch recall, reboot behavior and long sessions require separate checks. Keep USB and power connected during transfers; preserve logs and follow the bundled safety instructions after any failure. Custom effects can cause crackling, overflow, freezing or permanent device failure.

For audio comparisons, keep input signals and gain settings consistent and use independent recordings with level-matched A/B listening. Conversion scores alone do not establish fidelity; declared DSP load is scheduling metadata rather than measured utilization.
